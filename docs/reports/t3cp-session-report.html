The chain that was broken

Five defects, each one upstream of the last. Fixing any single one alone would have changed nothing visible.

B18

Drafting threw away every table and every emphasis marker

Fixed

_strip_inline() — "Drop markdown emphasis markers for v1 (no inline_formats yet)" 1,010 atom nodes across 224 atoms, of exactly four types: text_block 845 · bulleted_list 144 · numbered_list 19 · heading 2

The converter deleted **bold** and *italic* on the way through and handled no table, quote, rule or code fence. The editor and all four exporters could render every one of those perfectly — the content just never arrived carrying any.

B19

Every generated volume shipped with no header and no page numbers

Fixed

Technical Volume header NO · footer NO Cover / CCR / Supporting header NO · footer NO Cost Volume header yes · footer yes ← the only one

Molds spread a preset with header: null, and a mold's canvas wins at assembly — so the fallback that does carry furniture was unreachable for everything molded. The export routes were already passing {company_name} and turning {n}/{N} into live page fields. The whole mechanism worked and nothing was using it.

B21

"Full draft" silently skipped every registry-molded section

Fixed

Mode C reported "sections: 2" on a 14-section proposal 507 words per section — 500 of them inside [brackets] (threshold: 25)

The gate deciding "does this still need writing" answered already written about an untouched mold, because its word counter read bracketed instructions and form-field labels as prose. Now: a whole block that is one bracketed instruction is scaffolding, and a table's cells are a form's labels — what makes a form filled is values, not words.

B22

AI drafting replaced the solicitation's page cap with a preset's

Fixed

select page_allocation, canvas.max_pages from proposal_sections … 10 | 15 ← ten rows, all identical

Compliance correctness, not cosmetics: the editor gauge and the export floor both then measured against a limit the solicitation never gave, so a 14-page volume passes every check in the product and is refused by the agency.

B23

Ten items each carried the whole volume's page limit

Fixed

volume_required_items: 10 items × page_limit 10 (volume cap: 10) proposal_artifacts.compliance_spec.max_pages = 100

The build-out repeated the volume's "NTE 10 pages" onto every item, and provisioning summed them — so the export gate would have passed a hundred-page volume. Drafting to those allowances produced 27 pages against a 10-page cap. Readiness now blocks when the per-section allowances over-subscribe the volume: unlike an under-filled volume, that is arithmetic, and it cannot be what the solicitation meant.

The distinction that took three wrong answers to find

Chasing B22 made the volume swing from 4/15 to 27/10 to 6/1 before it was clear that three different numbers were being conflated: canvas.max_pages is the volume's cap, page_allocation is a section's share of it, and the preset is neither. My own first fix substituted the share for the cap — wrong in the opposite direction to the bug. A one-page share is not a one-page document.

What I did not close

Things I could have made look finished and did not. Each is reported by the product itself now, rather than being invisible.

GAP 1

The Technical Volume sits at 60% of its page envelope

Honest limit

The tenant's 224 atoms are whole prior proposals atomized as single documents, so retrieval genuinely runs dry — the ranker returns nothing further. Padding it would be exactly the hand-papering the brief forbids. The fix is library grain: atomizing those documents into sections. Readiness now says so out loud instead of the volume just being quietly short.

GAP 2

No figures anywhere in the narrative volumes

Honest limit

The PDF reader does not extract embedded images; images enter the library through the human box tool by design, which an unattended run cannot drive. What the system can produce it now does: the cost volume plots its own burden waterfall and work split as native chart nodes computed from the same numbers its tables print, so figure and table can never disagree.

B17

Two "reviewed" verdicts asserted without evidence the reviewer ran

Open — scoped

recorded_by: "advisory_manager" is a hardcoded literal, and one of these verdicts auto-advances a portal stage. The record step must stay independent of the AI step or a dead agent dead-ends the workflow — so the fix needs a third signal carrying "did the cohort actually run". That is a change to the step contract, not a patch, and I would not smuggle it into a bug-fix commit.

One verification I had to withdraw

I first reported the PDFs clean of foreign solicitation numbers on the strength of a pdftotext scan. poppler-utils is not installed here, so that command returned empty — proving nothing. The check was redone against the canvas in the database, which is authoritative: no foreign identifiers, own identifier OSW26BZ04-DP013 present.
