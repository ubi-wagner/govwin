RFP Pipeline · Investor Overview 01 / 05
The problem

Billions in non-dilutive funding go unclaimed — a path problem, not a money problem.

1

Discovery is noise

Thousands of solicitations across dozens of agencies and portals. The right one is a needle in a haystack — and deadlines pass unseen.

2

The blank page

Even a strong company stalls at a blank Volume I — 20–50 pages of tightly-specified, compliance-bound technical writing.

3

Compliance is pass / fail

Miss a required section, a page limit, a format rule — and a fundable idea is thrown out unread, before the science is ever evaluated.

4

Support doesn't scale

EconDev orgs, accelerators & PTACs want whole portfolios of SMBs to win — but hand-holding each proposal one-by-one doesn't scale.

Program scale is public and defensible — the friction between a capable company and that capital is the opportunity.
