RFP Pipeline is a multi-tenant platform for the small businesses chasing federal non-dilutive funding — and the economic-development organizations that back them. Expert curators turn raw solicitations into a ranked, personalized feed, pre-build the compliant skeleton for every opportunity, and let AI draft each section into that skeleton from your own reusable content. You review, collaborate, lock sections, and download a submission-ready Word volume — never starting from a blank page.
