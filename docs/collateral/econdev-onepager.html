The problem

SBIR money left on the table

Your companies have the ideas. What they lack is a proposal team — so non-dilutive dollars go unclaimed.

  • No proposal team. A Phase I volume runs 15–20 compliance-bound pages — and the founder already wears every hat.
  • The blank page. Even strong companies stall at "where do I start?" until the deadline passes.
  • Compliance risk. One missed requirement — a page limit, a required section — disqualifies a proposal before it is scored.
How RFP Pipeline helps

Expert skeletons, not DIY search

The moat is what we pre-build — and what your companies never have to face alone.

  • Curated skeletons. We pre-build the compliance matrix, section outline, and canvas "mold" templates for every required document — no blank page.
  • A ranked feed. Up to five Spotlight lenses per company (agency · technology · program), one scored card per opportunity.
  • A learning library. Company docs atomize into tagged, reusable content atoms — and winning drafts return for the next proposal.
  • Compliance baked in. AI drafts into the mold; a live matrix tracks completeness; the result exports as a submission-ready Word doc.
How it works

From opportunity to submission in three steps

A personalized, ranked feed of federal opportunities across each company's agency, technology, and program lenses.
Experts pre-build the compliance matrix and section molds; AI drafts each section from the learning library and the live RFP.
Collaborate with stage-scoped roles — including your EconDev shadow — lock sections, and export a submission-ready Word doc.
