How it works — the pursuit lifecycle

Apply · Curate · Draft · Win

One connected flow from market to award. You stay in control at every gate; the platform and its AI do the heavy lifting in between.

01

Apply — find the right opportunity

Daily, expert-curated ingestion across SAM.gov, SBIR.gov, Grants.gov, and agency portals — ranked to your tech areas. Deadline alerts keep you ahead of the clock. Pin a fit to pull its documents in, then open a proposal portal.

Export a compliant, submission-ready package in the agency’s format. Record the outcome — a win starts your contract with a kickoff task, and every result feeds your library so winning content ranks higher on the next bid.

A compounding advantage. Because everything you draft is captured to your library with lineage, your second proposal is faster than your first — and your tenth is faster than your second.

Discover & prioritize

Capabilities · 1 of 5

Opportunity discovery & your ranked pipeline

Every federal opportunity the platform surfaces lands on your Opportunity Pipeline, ranked for you. Each card shows the agency, program type, close date, and a live submission-stage badge — pre-release, open, updated, or closed.

How it works: daily ingestion across SAM.gov, SBIR.gov, Grants.gov, and agency portals; AI ranks each opportunity against your company profile and tech areas; deadline alerts fire before the clock runs out; pin an opportunity to copy its solicitation documents straight into your workspace.

Scoring buckets — rank by your strategy

Define scoring “buckets” — your own lenses on the market — from keywords, agencies, program types, and NAICS codes. Each bucket ranks your entire pipeline by your rules, so the opportunities that fit your strategy always rise to the top.

How it works: transparent, per-factor scoring you can inspect (you see why something ranked where it did); edit a bucket and the pipeline re-ranks instantly; keep multiple buckets for different pursuit strategies side by side.

Deadline & amendment awareness

The pipeline tracks the submission clock and flags solicitations that change. When an opportunity moves to Updated, you see it — and a resync pulls the new documents into your workspace.

How it works: submission-stage badges and due dates on every card; an “update available” strip when the agency revises a solicitation you’re pursuing; alerts surfaced on your dashboard and notification bell so a late change never slips past you.

Expert curation & isolated AI

Capabilities · 2 of 5

Expert-curated proposal workspaces

When you commit to a pursuit, an RFP Pipeline expert curates the solicitation and provisions your build — the compliance matrix, the required volumes, and the section molds — from the actual solicitation. You start with exactly the structure the agency expects: unlocked, populated, and ready to write.

How it works: a real expert reads the solicitation and builds the skeleton within a 72-hour SLA; provisioning instantiates the compliance matrix + volume molds; you also get a running Ask-the-Expert allowance for the judgment calls AI can’t make.

Your isolated, company-specific AI

Your AI is walled to your company. It drafts from your library and your context — and nothing you write trains a shared model or leaks to another company. It’s an advantage that compounds for you alone.

How it works: per-company isolation enforced at the data layer; no model training on your content; structured memory grounds drafts in your prior work; untrusted external content is fenced away from the AI so it can’t be steered by a malicious document.

Compliance built in from the first draft

The compliance matrix is populated the moment your build is provisioned and advances as you lock sections — so readiness is something you can see, not guess.

How it works: requirements are extracted from the solicitation into a live matrix; an AI compliance pass scores your proposal against them and flags gaps before an evaluator would; page-fill gauges track you against the agency’s limits.

Your library & workspace

Capabilities · 3 of 5

A reusable content library that compounds

Build a living library from your best work. Upload a document and the platform breaks it into tagged, searchable content pieces you compose into new proposals; capture content from any screen; or turn a past winning proposal into a reusable template.

How it works: upload → auto-atomize → tag → reuse; capture-from-screen pulls in content one-way without connecting an account; your content is copied forward into each proposal, so retiring an item never disturbs a proposal already built from it.

The proposal workspace & compliance matrix

The build cockpit: a volume-and-section matrix, a stage-control bar with visible gate requirements, per-section accept-and-lock, and page-fill gauges. Advance a stage only when its gates are met.

How it works: sections carry status, assignee, and a page-fill bar; accept & lock a section to freeze it; when a volume is fully locked its downloads light up; the live compliance matrix advances with every lock.

Templates, versioning & a full audit trail

Reuse a winning structure as a template for the next bid, keep a version history on every section, and see a complete, attributed record of who did what — human or AI.

How it works: save any proposal’s structure as a reusable skeleton; per-section version history with restore; every action posts to an audit trail, so nothing happens off the record.

The AI workforce

Capabilities · 4 of 5

An AI workforce works your proposal alongside your team — always advisory, always landing in review for your approval. It never submits, locks, or advances a stage on its own, and it’s governed by budget, rate, and per-run cost caps you set.

Proposal Studio

Runs your draft through three gated loops — Draft, Refine, Compliance — stopping at each gate for your review, comments, and approval, or running all three automatically when you want speed.

AI drafting & full-draft modes

First-draft every empty section from your library, or draft the whole proposal in one pass — guided, restyle-only, or full-auto — in the voice and tone you choose.

Color-team review

Runs a red/gold-team pass and posts specific, section-level recommendations into each section’s thread for your team to accept or set aside.

Compliance check

Scores your proposal against the solicitation’s requirements, pass / fail / partial per item, so you fix gaps before an evaluator finds them.

Research scout

Gathers market research, prior art, and the competitor landscape — including federal sources — into a cited brief for your review. Web results are treated as untrusted data.

Cost model

Assembles your cost volume with live formulas, so the numbers stay consistent, traceable, and defensible.

Advisory by design. Every AI output — Studio, drafting, review, or a full draft — lands in review, redlined and reversible. Your team is the gate; the AI never advances past it on its own.

Collaborate, deliver & win

Team & partner collaboration

Invite teammates and external partners with per-section, per-permission access — view, comment, or edit — so everyone sees exactly what they should and nothing more.

How it works: grants are per section and per permission; segregated collaboration spaces wall off partner content; every contributor’s work is attributed; deactivating a member revokes access instantly while keeping their history.

Submission-ready deliverables

Export your proposal as Word, PDF, Excel (cost volumes with live formulas), or a per-volume ZIP — submission-formatted, with figures and tables rendered natively and sections in true document order.

How it works: the same engine renders every format from one canvas; a packaging review compiles a manifest — volume completeness, required forms, and page/format compliance — before you submit; downloads unlock once the proposal is locked.

Outcome & contract — the flywheel

Record the outcome of every pursuit. A win starts your contract with a kickoff task in your queue, and every outcome feeds your library so winning content ranks higher on your next bid.

How it works: record Won / Lost / Withdrawn once submitted; a win instantiates a contract entity + kickoff; outcome signals re-weight your library so the platform gets sharper the more you use it.
