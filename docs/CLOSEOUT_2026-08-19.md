## 2. What was fixed, and what proved it

### B17 — a "reviewed" verdict asserted with no evidence a reviewer ran
The record step that stamps `reviewed` is independent of the AI cohort *by design* — an advisory
agent must never dead-end a customer's workflow — so it asserted the verdict from a literal. A
cohort that safe-skipped for want of an API key was indistinguishable from one that ran and found
nothing, and on the portal gate "found nothing" is what authorizes an **automatic stage advance**.
Silence passed the gate.

The third signal is **execution provenance, not agent output**: the engine's own record of what
each `AI_INVOKE` step did, injected only into actions that declare `_ai_step_status`. An action can
learn that the reviewer ran; it still cannot learn what the reviewer said, so the
input-map-ancestor invariant stands and the record step still declares no `depends_on`.

**Driving it live caught the deeper half** (→ B30): the engine writes `completed` for any step whose
executor returned a value, and a safe-skip returns one. Reading the status string alone would have
re-shipped the same lie in evidence-shaped packaging. The evidence now comes off the step *result*.

Proof: `pipeline/scripts/drive_b17_evidence.py` — the real engine, real DB, one workflow, two runs.
Fabric absent ⇒ `not_reviewed` and the summary says so; fabric present ⇒ `reviewed` and the evidence
names the step. Both runs complete.

### B31 — a corpus regex that matched the letter "s"
`'\s+'` inside a **JavaScript template literal** is `'s+'`. Postgres was replacing every run of the
letter S in the solicitation corpus rather than whitespace, so identical text could not match
itself. The failure was silent and read as good news: the backfill reported *"0 atoms are the
agency's words"* across 301 atoms.

Found only because the live drive gave the detector something real to find. A checker whose
"nothing found" is indistinguishable from "never ran" is not proven until a fixture makes it fire.

### LIB-HYGIENE — agency boilerplate is fenced by WHOSE words it is
Atomizing a solicitation package fills a tenant's library with the agency's instruction text. The
first fix attempt was structural — exclude anything under the reference folder — and it was wrong:
that folder also holds the tenant's uploaded past proposals and every harvested figure, which is
the best material they own (measured: their own section 4.39 against the boilerplate's 1.06).
Reverted.

The property that actually separates them is not where a file sits but who wrote it, and that has a
deterministic test: **text appearing verbatim in the shared solicitation corpus was written by the
agency.** Mig 197 stamps it at creation; retrieval drops it. It is a fence, not a delete — the atom
stays in the library and stays insertable by hand.

### Page furniture, measured on all 39 molds
`scripts/sweep-mold-quality.mts` renders every mold through the product's own PDF exporter and
counts what Chromium laid out. The first run flagged 22 of 39 — and most of that was **the check
being wrong**. A slide deck has no running header; a one-page letter of collaboration with "Page 1
of 1" reads as machine-made. With the rule split by type, **3 molds were genuinely bare** and are
now fixed. 39 of 39 clean.

### The CMS drive was breaking its own test
`close-e2e-cms.mjs` archived the active guide and assumed a draft was waiting — true the first
time, false ever after. Run two archived the live guide, found nothing to publish, reported the
publish path broken, **and left a guide off the public marketing site.** Fixed and re-run: draft →
Publish as a real admin → active v4 → `/resources/what-is-a-baa` returns 200.

## 4. Still open — three things, stated rather than buried

**1. The visual reviewer's eyesight is unproven.** The page-count half is live everywhere and needs
no key — it renders and counts, and it already caught a volume shipping 11 pages against a 10-page
cap. The **vision** half needs a real `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`; the emulator cannot see images and
returns `[]` deliberately, because a harness that invented plausible defects would make the review
look proven when nothing had looked at a page. The wire is proven end to end; the seeing is not.

**2. Drafted prose is limited by the harness, not by the pipeline.** The T3CP Technical Volume
fills its envelope — 10 rendered pages against a 10-page cap, ~36,350 characters against the
reference proposal's 36,701, two of the company's own photographs placed and captioned. That is the
*shape* being right. The sentences are composed by the sandbox emulator from retrieved library
text, so prose quality is the one axis this environment cannot measure. Everything else in the path
is identical between emulator and production. Tracked as **#189**.

**3. B30 — the engine records a safe-skipped step as "completed".** Deliberately not fixed today.
`depends_on` treats `skipped` as a reason to skip dependents, so correcting the stored status
cascades through the full-draft and Studio workflows. That may well be right, but it is a semantics
change to the engine with real blast radius and belongs in its own change with its own drive. B17
is closed without it. The visible cost meanwhile: `/admin/workflows` shows a green "completed" AI
step that never called an agent.

`docs/BUG_LOG_2026-08-19.md` — **30 entries, 0 open.** B17 closed today; B30 logged as a deliberate
deferral with its blast radius written down; B31 logged with its class (*a backslash escape inside
a JS template literal meant for another language* — SQL regexes, `LIKE` escapes, hand-built JSON).

Two lessons repeated themselves today and are worth carrying forward:

- **A pure-function test proves the function, never the wire.** B17's deep half and B31 were both
  invisible to green unit tests and both fell out of the first live drive.
- **A checker that finds nothing has not been proven to work.** Give it something to find first.
