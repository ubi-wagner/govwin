# RFP Pipeline — Customer Guidebook
### Building a winning proposal, end to end — worked with Foundation's Ohio TVSF (Round 45) application

*Prepared for **Paul Jackson**, Entrepreneurs' Center — a walkthrough of the whole customer
journey, from your reusable content library to a submitted, downloadable proposal. Every screen
below is the real product, driven live on the Foundation workspace.*

> **The five-minute version:** stock your **Library** once → the platform shows you the
> **opportunities that fit you**, ranked by your interests → you **buy a build** for the one you
> want → an RFP expert **curates & releases** it → the **AI drafts** each required section from your
> library → you **review, lock, and submit** → you **download** the finished proposal + budget.

---

## 1 · Build your Library (do this once, reuse forever)
Your Library is your company's reusable content — company overview, technology, past performance,
team bios, budget basis. You stock it by **uploading documents** or **pasting content**, and the
platform *deconstructs* each source into small, tagged, selectable objects ("atoms").

![Your Library](./ui-walkthrough/00a_library.png)

**Atomize** — paste (or upload) a source document and click **Deconstruct**; the platform detects
paragraphs, figures, tables and lists, and you keep only the pieces you want, tagged against one
taxonomy so they're findable later.

![Atomize a document](./ui-walkthrough/00c_atomize_deconstructed.png)

Bulk‑loading a whole prior proposal? Use **Upload package** — it atomizes the entire document into
your library in one pass (and can be "templified" into a reusable structure for a fresh draft).

![Upload a package](./ui-walkthrough/00d_upload_package.png)

---

## 2 · Find & select your opportunity
Your **Opportunity Pipeline** is *ranked by your spotlight buckets* — the interest areas you care
about (for Foundation: additive construction, advanced manufacturing, construction tech, materials,
non‑dilutive capital). Each card shows how it scores against each bucket, so the best‑fit work rises
to the top. Here Foundation sees its **TVSF** opportunity alongside four SBIR/STTR options, each
scored. Click **Build →** on the one you want to pursue.

![Pipeline, ranked by your buckets](./ui-walkthrough/01_pipeline_select_tvsf.png)

---

## 3 · Start the build (purchase)
Pin the opportunity, then **Purchase** opens the workspace. Enter your access/comp code and click
**Complete purchase** — no card needed for a comped build.

![Purchase with a code](./ui-walkthrough/02_purchase_modal.png)

Your build is now **curation pending** — an RFP expert prepares the compliance shell for you
(a 72‑hour service window).

![Curation pending](./ui-walkthrough/03_curation_pending.png)

---

## 4 · Curation & release (handled by an RFP expert)
An RFP expert enters your workspace (fully audited), confirms the solicitation's required volumes and
sections, and clicks **Release to customer** — which provisions your editable build and instantiates
the compliance matrix from the solicitation.

![RFP expert releases the build](./ui-walkthrough/04b_released_provisioned.png)

---

## 5 · Your build workspace
Your proposal opens **unlocked**, laid out exactly to the solicitation: here the **Proposal** volume
(Abstract + the 11 TVSF questions) and the **Budget** volume (#12), with a **Draft → Final** stage
gate and per‑section controls.

![The build workspace](./ui-walkthrough/05_build_workspace_empty.png)

---

## 6 · Draft with AI (your agents, your library)
Click **Draft All Sections** and the AI section‑drafter writes each required section — grounded in
**your library atoms** and the opportunity's context — so the first draft is yours, not generic.

![The AI section drafter](./ui-walkthrough/06a_ai_drafter_panel.png)

Every section comes back **complete**, with block counts, ready to review — the Proposal's 12
sections and the Budget's spend‑type table.

![All sections drafted](./ui-walkthrough/07a_sections_drafted.png)

---

## 7 · Check compliance
The **Compliance** tab tracks every solicitation requirement against your sections, and can run an
AI compliance check — so nothing required is missed before you submit.

![Compliance checklist](./ui-walkthrough/08_compliance_matrix.png)

---

## 8 · Lock & submit
Review each section, then **Accept & Lock** (the compliance matrix flips to *satisfied*), and
**Advance** the stage. At the final gate the whole proposal is locked and **submitted**.

![Locked & submitted](./ui-walkthrough/10_advanced_submitted.png)

---

## 9 · Download your finished proposal
With the proposal locked, **Download Proposal (.docx)** produces the final document (and the Budget
exports as .xlsx). That's your submission‑ready package.

![Download the completed proposal](./ui-walkthrough/11a_completed_ready_to_download.png)

**Deliverables:** `Foundation_TVSF_Proposal.docx` (Abstract + questions #1–#11) ·
`Foundation_TVSF_Budget.xlsx` ($200,000 spend‑type budget). Both are in
`docs/proposals/foundation-tvsf/`.
