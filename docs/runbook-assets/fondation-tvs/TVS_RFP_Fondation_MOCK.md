# TVS — Technology Validation & Startup Solicitation (MOCK)

**Issuing body (mock):** State Economic Development Office — Third Frontier Commercialization Program
**Program:** Technology Validation & Startup (TVS) Fund
**Solicitation No. (mock):** TVS-2026-01
**Format version:** Econ-Dev Format v1 (NON-federal; not SBIR/STTR — see §2)
**Open date:** 2026-08-01  ·  **Close date:** 2026-10-15  *(both required to publish — see playbook)*

---

## 1. Program overview (Econ-Development guidance)

The TVS Fund accelerates the **commercialization of validated technology** by Ohio-based companies
and university spinouts, with the explicit goal of **in-state economic impact** — jobs created/retained,
follow-on private investment leveraged, and new products reaching market. Unlike a federal SBIR/STTR
award, TVS is a **state cost-share grant**: the award must be matched by non-state funds, and the
evaluation weighs **economic-impact and leverage** as heavily as technical merit.

Two tracks (an applicant selects ONE):
- **Track A — Technology Validation:** de-risk a specific technology to a defined validation milestone.
- **Track B — Startup:** stand up / scale an Ohio company to commercialize a validated technology.

---

## 2. What makes this a NEW format (vs the standard SBIR/STTR molds)

| Dimension | Standard SBIR/STTR | **TVS (Econ-Dev Format v1)** |
|---|---|---|
| Budget basis | Federal fee/indirect (fringe/OH/G&A/fee) | **State grant + required non-state MATCH** (≥ 1:1) |
| Extra required volume | — | **Ohio Economic-Impact Statement** (jobs, follow-on investment) |
| Commercialization | Phase-II plan | **Commercialization & Market-Entry Plan** (required at award) |
| Support letters | LOIs/LOS | **Binding Match-Commitment Letters** (non-state funds) |
| Compliance flavor | ITAR/CUI, page limits, fonts | No ITAR/CUI; **Ohio-nexus attestation**; milestone-based deliverables |
| PoP | 6/12/18/24-mo, base+option | **12–24 month validation timeline** with milestone gates |

## 3. Required application volumes (the format to curate)

Curate these as the opportunity's **volumes → required items → compliance matrix** (playbook Phase C).

| # | Volume | Required items (each a matrix row) | Page/format limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| V1 | **Cover & Eligibility** | Applicant legal name · EIN · Ohio location attestation · UEI · Track (A/B) · Amount requested · Match amount & source | Form (fielded), PDF |
| V2 | **Project Narrative** | Technology description · Validation/commercialization objective · Work plan & milestones · Related work · Team & facilities | 12 pages, PDF |
| V3 | **Commercialization & Market-Entry Plan** | Target market & customers · Path to revenue · IP position · Competitive landscape | 6 pages, PDF/PPT |
| V4 | **Budget & Match** | Line-item budget (personnel, equipment, services, ODC) · **Match sources & amounts** · Cost-share schedule | Sheet (XLSX) → PDF |
| V5 | **Ohio Economic-Impact Statement** | Jobs created/retained (year 1–3) · Follow-on private investment leveraged · In-state spend | 2 pages, PDF |
| V6 | **Supporting Documents** | **Binding match-commitment letter(s)** · Key-personnel bios · Facilities/RI agreements (Track A) | PDF attachments |

**Evaluation criteria (weights, mock):** Technical merit & feasibility (25) · Commercialization potential (25) ·
**Ohio economic impact & leverage (25)** · Team & execution capability (15) · Budget realism & match (10).

**Submission:** single combined PDF per volume, uploaded to the program portal by the close date.
Milestone-based disbursement; a mid-term validation gate at month 12.
