Contribute to a Proposal, Scoped to You

You were invited to help on one proposal. This guide is your complete manual: accept the invite, find your assigned sections, contribute in the canvas, comment, move files, and understand exactly what each grant unlocks.

Collaborators are stage-scoped — you see exactly what you were invited to, and nothing else.

Orientation — a scoped collaborator

where invite email → /portal/<company>/proposals

You’ve been invited to help on one proposal at one company. This guide is your whole world in the platform: what you can open, how you contribute, and where your assignments live. Collaborators are stage-scoped — your access is granted per section and per permission (view, comment, or edit).

There are two kinds of invite. An External invite makes you a partner_user — the scoped collaborator this guide covers. A Contributor invite makes you internal staff (tenant-wide). If you see the full company sidebar, you were invited as a Contributor; if you see only Proposals, you’re an External collaborator.

Accept your invite & sign in

where /invite/<token> → /change-password → the proposal

Your invite arrives as a deep-linked email from the company that wants your help.

A revoked invite shows “This invitation is no longer valid.” An already-used one shows Invite Already Accepted with a Go to Login button. First sign-in on a fresh account asks you to change your temporary password before anything else.

  1. Follow the email link to /invite/<token>. The Accept Invitation page names the inviter, the proposal, the company, and your email.
  2. Set a Password (at least 12 characters) and Confirm Password, then Set Password & Accept. You’re dropped straight into the proposal workspace.
  3. If you’re an existing user, you’re auto-accepted and the email links you straight to the proposal — no password step.

Your landing — only your assigned work

where /portal/<company>/proposals

Unlike a customer admin, your home is the proposals list scoped to you. The sidebar shows only Proposals (plus the notification bell and Sign out) — no dashboard, pipeline, library, or billing.

Navigating to a company screen you’re not scoped for (dashboard, library, buckets, billing) simply redirects you back to Proposals. Nothing breaks — you’re gently kept in your lane.

  1. Open the proposal you were invited to. Inside, the workspace defaults to the My Sections tab — the sections assigned to you, each with a permission pill (edit / comment / view).
  2. If nothing’s assigned yet, you’ll see “No sections are assigned to you yet.” until an admin adds you.

Inside the proposal — your view

where /portal/<company>/proposals/<id>

The proposal workspace, scoped to you, is organized into what you can act on.

The Contributor View groups your work: My Assignments (or Shared With Me for externals) — the sections you can edit, each with a status badge, page progress, and Open Editor →; My Dropbox — your private file drop for this proposal; Add content — offer a document into the company library; Sections (Comment Only) with View & Comment; and Other Sections (View Only) with View. A Timeline tab shows the proposal’s activity feed.

If the agency amends the solicitation, an amber banner appears at the top of the workspace with the change. You can read it (and expand the compliance delta), but only a company admin can acknowledge it — “An admin must acknowledge these changes.” Re-check any section you’re drafting against the change.

  1. Open a section you can edit and work in the same canvas editor the customer uses — headings, text, lists, tables, images as structured nodes.
  2. Your edits save to the shared draft. The section owner reviews and, when it’s right, Accepts & Locks it — that’s an admin action; your job is to get the content right and save.

Comment & suggest

where section canvas → Node / Review tab

Comments are how you contribute without changing the draft directly. They thread per section (surfaced in the Node tab when a node is selected, and in the dedicated Review tab).

Resolving is one-way (there’s no un-resolve), and comments can’t be edited or deleted — so post a fresh comment to add or correct something.

  1. Type in Add comment… and press Enter or click Add. A “reply” is simply another comment on the same section thread.
  2. Click Resolve (on a human comment) or Dismiss (on an 🤖 AI Review item). Resolved comments collapse under a Show/Hide N resolved toggle.

Files — dropbox & add content

where section workspace → My Dropbox · Add content

Two ways to move files.

My Dropbox is your private per-proposal file drop — you see only your own files (delete your own with the ×). Add content lets you offer a document into the company library: drop a file and it’s atomized (“Atomized N files into M atoms. Review in Library.”). The upload works even though the Library link itself is out of your scope — an admin reviews what you contributed.

Uploads are blocked once the proposal is locked (you’ll get a clear message). Before the first lock, section export is also unavailable (“Downloads available after final review and lock”).

Collaboration vaults (“nooks”) — your shared workspace

where /vaults

A nook is a private, segregated workspace between you and a customer — a place to hand content back and forth without either side seeing the other’s wider library. It is separate from proposal collaboration: if a customer invites you into a nook, you reach it at /vaults (the platform routes you there automatically at sign-in when you have no proposal assignment). You see only the nook(s) you were invited to — never the customer’s main library, and never another partner’s nook.

When you add content, the customer is notified and a review task is raised on their side — they decide what (if anything) to pull into their proposal. Only add content you’re comfortable with the customer using.

A nook is deliberately quiet: no main-library clutter, no other partners. Everything you add stays scoped to that one customer relationship.

  1. Sign in. If your only access is a nook, you land on /vaults — a simple list of the customer(s) you collaborate with. Click Open nook → to enter.
  2. Add an artifact — name it, pick its form (Document · Deck · Sheet · PDF), and add it. Your upload is atomized into the nook (it is invisible to the customer’s main library until they choose to harvest it).
  3. Download a whole artifact in any format (.docx · .pptx · .xlsx · .pdf). You can download whole artifacts only — pulling out individual sections is a customer-side action.

Scope boundaries — what’s out of reach

where redirects / access checks

By design, a collaborator can’t see the company’s book of business. This keeps their pipeline, library, and billing private while still letting you do the work you were brought in for.

Access is a living grant. An admin can widen it (view → comment → edit), narrow it, or revoke it. If your access ends, your collaborator membership at that company is cleanly removed — without touching any other company you collaborate with. Re-inviting a revoked email restores the same record with fresh grants.
