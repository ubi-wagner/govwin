## NILOC Technologies — capability statement (core)

**Who we are.** NILOC Technologies is a small business that licenses federally-developed
innovations and matures them into fielded products. We partner with DoD laboratories and the
federal technology-transfer ecosystem (TechLink and lab T2 offices) to identify lab-proven
technology, de-risk it under SBIR/STTR and BAA/OTA vehicles, and commercialize it for defense and
dual-use markets.

**Core competencies.**
- Federal technology transfer & licensing (TechLink / DoD T2) → commercialization.
- RF / electromagnetics; computer vision & AI/ML; autonomy & systems; advanced materials.
- AI-accelerated proposal development and program execution (via our RFP Pipeline platform).
- SBIR/STTR, BAA, OTA/CSO capture, compliant proposal authoring, and Phase II→transition planning.

**Differentiators.**
- A licensing-and-commercialization model that starts from lab-validated IP — lower technical risk,
  faster to a credible Phase II.
- AI-native execution: RFP Pipeline compresses proposal and program-management cycle time.
